Analytics records only from the day it is installed. Every week it is not on the site is a week of history you can never get back — and it is free.

For somebody who wants to evaluate things on evidence, this is the binding constraint. You cannot judge a marketing company, including us, without a baseline. Right now there is nothing to compare an "after" against.

This is also the first thing we would do, before proposing anything at all — because we would rather be judged on measured numbers than on a good conversation.

Finding 2 — Your Business Is Already Built For The Market That Now Exists
This is the most valuable thing in this document and you may not have noticed it

The federal residential solar credit ended for systems placed in service after 31 December 2025. Across the country, companies whose entire model was selling new residential installations on that 30% argument are in serious trouble — several large ones have gone bankrupt.

You do not sell that. Read your own service list back:

Panel cleaning & ceramic coating no credit involved Maintenance & monitoring no credit involved Inverter upgrades no credit involved Removal & reinstall for roofing no credit involved Additional panels upgrade to an existing owner Battery storage the one area still strongly supported

Every line of that is work on systems that already exist, bought by people who already own solar. The tax change did not touch your business model. It damaged your competitors'.

And Florida has a very large installed base of systems put in during the boom years, many of them sold by companies that no longer exist. Those systems are now five to ten years old, dirty, occasionally faulting, and due roof replacements. That is your market and it grows on its own.

We are not tax advisers and this is not tax advice — confirm the position with your CPA. But commercially, the point stands.

Finding 3 — A Gmail Address On A Domain You Own
Small, and it costs more than it looks
Published email genesissolarsystems1@gmail.com Domain you own genesissolarsystems.com

You own the domain, so ean@genesissolarsystems.com costs nothing extra. On a $300 cleaning job nobody minds. On a battery quote worth five figures, or a commercial enquiry, a gmail address is the detail that makes a cautious buyer hesitate.

There is a second, more practical reason. Email sent from a free address to a business is more likely to be filtered, and we understand a previous message to this business bounced. Mail on your own domain, configured properly, is more reliable as well as more credible.

Finding 4 — One Form, And “By Appointment Only”
Where the enquiries are going

You have one form and a tappable number, and the site says by appointment only. That is honest, and for a service business it is reasonable — but it means everybody who lands outside working hours has one option: remember you tomorrow.

Service work is a repeat purchase and nothing on the site asks for the second one. Panels in north Florida need cleaning more than once a year. A customer you cleaned for last spring is the cheapest job you will ever win, and there is currently no reminder, no schedule and no reason for them to come back to you rather than search again.

That is the highest-return change available to you, and it costs less than any advertising.

Finding 5 — Your Images Are 3.4 Megabytes Each
Measured directly — and this one you will appreciate
dirty-panel.jpg 3,457,412 bytes (3.4 MB) solar-work.jpg 3,399,274 bytes (3.4 MB) scenic-under.jpg 1,706,508 bytes (1.7 MB) Three images 8.5 MB before a word of text loads

A photograph displayed a few hundred pixels wide does not need to be 3.4 megabytes. Properly sized, each of those would be under 200KB with no visible difference — roughly a 95% reduction.

Why it matters commercially, not just technically. A large share of your visitors are on a phone, some of them on mobile data in a rural part of Leon or Gadsden county. Every second of load time loses people, and page speed is one of the factors search engines weigh. You are paying for that weight on every single visit.

It is also the cheapest fix in this document — a few minutes of work, no design change, and unlike everything else here you would be able to measure the difference immediately once analytics is on.

What Is Genuinely Good
The positioning is better than the measurement
  • A clear, specific service list — cleaning, ceramic coating, maintenance, monitoring, inverter upgrades, additional panels, battery storage, and removal and reinstall for roof work.
  • Removal and reinstall is a genuinely smart line. Every roof replacement in Florida on a house with panels needs somebody to do it, most roofers will not touch it, and it comes with a deadline.
  • Battery storage — the part of the market with the strongest remaining support.
  • A working enquiry form and tap-to-call on 850-350-6165.
  • Named counties — Leon and Gadsden, plus Jacksonville.
  • Licensed and insured stated, with warranty language throughout.
  • A fast, modern site. Technically it is well built.

The strategy is right. The instrumentation is missing.
